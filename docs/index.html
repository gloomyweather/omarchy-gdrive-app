omarchy-gdrive

google drive as a normal folder on omarchy.

open, edit and save drive files from files, the terminal or any app. it runs only on your computer, built on rclone. there is no server and no account other than your own google one.

  • bookmarks in files for my drive, shared with me and shared drives
  • right-click: open in google drive, copy link, make available offline
  • a drive icon in the bar: connected, uploading, offline, error
  • omarchy menu entry and the super + ctrl + g hotkey
  • connects at login, waits for the network, finishes uploads at shutdown

01install

git clone https://github.com/gloomyweather/omarchy-gdrive-app.git ~/.local/src/omarchy-gdrive
~/.local/src/omarchy-gdrive/install.sh
nautilus -q

02create your google key

about ten minutes, once. follow the step-by-step guide. you end up with a client id and a client secret.

03connect

omarchy-gdrive setup

paste the client id and the secret, sign in to google, pick your drives. your drive is now in the files sidebar and in ~/GoogleDrive.
