privacy policy

last updated 2026-10-25

omarchy-gdrive is a program that runs on your own computer. it has no server, no analytics and no accounts. this page explains what it does with your google data: it keeps it between your computer and google.

01what it accesses

with your permission, omarchy-gdrive uses the google drive api to list, read, create, update and delete files in your google drive. that is what lets your drive appear as a folder on your computer.

02where your data goes

file contents and listings travel directly between google and the rclone program on your machine. cached copies of files stay on your disk. your sign-in token is stored on your disk in an encrypted rclone configuration file.

nothing is sent to the authors of omarchy-gdrive or to anyone else.

03what it never does

  • collect, sell, share or transfer your google data
  • use your data for advertising
  • use your data to train machine learning models

04google api services

omarchy-gdrive's use of information received from google apis adheres to the google api services user data policy, including the limited use requirements.

05removing access

revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. uninstalling with omarchy-gdrive uninstall --purge-config deletes the local token and settings; --purge-cache also deletes cached files.

06contact

questions about the software: open an issue at github.com/gloomyweather/omarchy-gdrive-app.
